# Product Requirement Document

## 01 — Problem Statement
Students in finance and business analytics often struggle to clearly communicate their skills, projects, and experience in a way that stands out to recruiters. Traditional resumes alone do not effectively showcase technical abilities, project outcomes, or leadership experience.

Colby Davis needs a centralized, professional landing page that highlights his data-driven skillset, project experience, and leadership background in a clean, recruiter-friendly format to increase his chances of securing internships and entry-level analyst roles.

## 02 — Target Audience
- Recruiters at mid-to-large companies
- Hiring for:
  - Business Analyst roles  
  - Financial Analyst roles  
  - Business Analytics / Data Analytics internships  
- Industries:
  - Finance  
  - Consulting  
  - Corporate analytics / business intelligence  

## 03 — Goals & Success Metrics

| Goal | How We Measure It |
|------|------------------|
| Increase recruiter engagement | Click-through rate to LinkedIn profile |
| Showcase project experience effectively | Time spent on Projects section |
| Communicate technical and analytical skills clearly | Recruiter ability to identify tools within 10 seconds |
| Improve internship opportunities | Number of recruiter contacts/interview requests |
| Provide easy access to professional materials | Resume downloads and LinkedIn visits |

## 04 — Required Content

### Hero Section
Must: Display value proposition and LinkedIn CTA  
Should: Include resume download CTA  

### About Me
Must: Education, GPA, majors, certificate  
Should: Career interests  

### Projects
Must: Include Movie Analytics App, Bicycle Simulation, Regression Project  
Should: Tools used  

### Skills
Must: Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Bloomberg, Python, SQL  
Should: Grouped categories  

### Experience & Leadership
Must: Child Care Provider, Referee, Delta Tau Delta finance roles  
Should: Emphasize leadership  

### Resume
Must: Downloadable PDF  
Should: Preview  

### Contact
Must: Email and LinkedIn  
Should: Contact form  

## 05 — Scope

| In Scope This Week | Out of Scope |
|-------------------|-------------|
| Single-page site | Multi-page architecture |
| Core sections | Blog system |
| LinkedIn & Resume CTAs | Backend systems |
| Mobile responsive | CMS |

## 06 — Assumptions & Constraints

Assumptions:
- Recruiters scan quickly  
- Projects and experience matter most  

Constraints:
- Static site  
- Limited time  
- Simple corporate design
